How does the plant identifier work?

Snap a photo of any houseplant or garden plant and PlantCare's plant recognition identifies the species in seconds, then builds a care plan around it — watering, light, feeding, and repotting.

Is PlantCare a garden app or a houseplant app?

Both. It's built for houseplant parents first — identify plants, track watering, diagnose disease — and works just as well for balcony and garden plants.
